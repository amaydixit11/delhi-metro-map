## Discrepancies to flag on the map
- Wiki Phase V(A) table says Magenta 8 stns; press says "13 new stations" total for V(A). Station-box counting explains the gap.
- Violet Line wiki says "no Violet extension in Phase 5", while the V(C) table lists Palwal.
- The Pink Gokulpuri–Arthala count is 8 in the table and 10 in the named list.
- V(C) also omits several items from the 18-corridor list: CGO Complex–Surya Vihar, Mayur Vihar III–Loni Border, and Yamuna Bank–Loni.
